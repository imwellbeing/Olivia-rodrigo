Who is Olivia Rodrigo?

Olivia Rodrigo is an American singer-songwriter who grew up in Temecula, California. Before music took over, she was known as an actor on Disney Channel’s Bizaardvark and later as Nini in High School Musical: The Musical: The Series.

In January 2021 she released “drivers license,” a piano ballad about a painful breakup. It debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 and turned her into a global name almost overnight. Her debut album, SOUR, followed that May, with Dan Nigro producing most of it.

At the 2022 Grammys she won Best New Artist, Best Pop Vocal Album and Best Pop Solo Performance.

The songs on SOUR

1brutal

Pop-punk opener

The album starts with a loud, anxious rant about the pressure of being a teenager who feels like she’s supposed to have life figured out. It sets the tone: funny, dramatic and very honest.

2traitor

Slow, accusing ballad

A quieter song aimed at an ex who moved on too fast. Instead of screaming, she sounds calm and hurt, which makes the accusation land harder.

3drivers license

The breakout hit

Released in January 2021, this piano ballad is built around a simple image: finally getting a license and having no one to share it with. It debuted at No. 1 on the Hot 100 and earned her a Grammy for Best Pop Solo Performance.

41 step forward, 3 steps back

Soft and confused

She describes a relationship where every bit of progress gets undone. The song borrows from Taylor Swift’s “New Year’s Day,” so Swift and Jack Antonoff are credited as writers.

5deja vu

Dreamy synth-pop

She imagines her ex repeating their old rituals with someone new, from the car rides to the little inside jokes. The bridge nods to Taylor Swift’s “Cruel Summer,” which is why Swift and Antonoff are credited here too.

6good 4 u

Pop-punk blast

Her angriest and most fun song: a sarcastic reaction to an ex who seems totally fine after the breakup. Its guitar energy reminded many listeners of Paramore, and Hayley Williams and Josh Farro received writing credits.

7enough for you

Quiet regret

A sad look back at changing herself to please someone who never appreciated it. It’s one of the album’s most vulnerable songs.

8happier

Bittersweet goodbye

She says she wants her ex to be okay, then admits she can’t stand the thought of them being happier with someone else. That honest, slightly selfish feeling is exactly what makes it relatable.

9jealousy, jealousy

Social media spiral

This one is about comparing yourself to other people online and feeling smaller for it. It stands out because it’s less about a breakup and more about self-esteem.

10favorite crime

Gentle and wistful

A soft song that looks back at a relationship with fondness and guilt, treating the whole thing like a crime she can’t stop thinking about.

11hope ur ok

Tender closer

The finale turns away from romance and toward friends and people from her past who didn’t get the care they needed. It ends the album on kindness instead of anger.

About GUTS

After SOUR made her a star, Olivia Rodrigo came back with GUTS on September 8, 2023. Dan Nigro produced it again, and the album debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200.

The lead single, “vampire,” arrived in June 2023 and debuted at No. 1 on the Hot 100. The record keeps the diary-style songwriting but adds more humor and more pop-punk energy, and it spends a lot of time on the pressure of being young, perfect and judged.

A fast, funny song about going back to an ex even though she knows she shouldn’t. It treats a bad decision like a guilty pleasure.

Awkward and chaotic

She replays a social disaster in her head, cringing over everything she said. It’s fast, messy and very relatable if you’ve ever overthought a night out.

6making the bed

Moody and self-aware

A reflective song where she admits that some of her problems are of her own making. It trades anger for honesty.

7logical

Gentle and heartbroken

Playful pop-punk

She can’t decide whether she wants revenge on her ex or wants him back, and the song happily admits it’s both. It’s one of the album’s most fun, shout-along moments.

9love is embarrassing

Bright and jittery

A song about the cringey, exposed feeling of falling for someone and doing silly things because of it.

11pretty isn’t pretty

About the album

Olivia Rodrigo released her third album on June 12, 2026, on Geffen Records. Dan Nigro produced most of it again, with extra help from Jim-E Stack, Noah Conrad and Mike Wise.

It swaps the guitar crunch of her earlier records for synths and ’80s new wave, and Robert Smith of The Cure is the only guest. The 13 songs are split into two sides, seven called “girl so in love” and six called “you seem pretty sad.” In the UK, it gave her the biggest opening week of her career.
